📚 Resources

A curated collection of tools, libraries, tutorials, artists, and communities for generative and computational art. Whether you're writing your first sketch or exploring new techniques, these are the places to look.

🔧 Libraries & Frameworks

Library

p5.js

The go-to creative coding library for JavaScript. Makes drawing, animation, and interaction trivially easy. A community fork of Processing for the web.

p5js.org Platform

OpenProcessing

Share and explore p5.js / Processing sketches online. Thousands of community sketches you can run, remix, and learn from.

openprocessing.org Library

Three.js

WebGL-powered 3D graphics library. Essential for generative 3D art, shader work, and real-time visualizations in the browser.

threejs.org Library

Poisson Disk Sampling

Blue-noise point distribution — the foundation of many pleasing generative compositions. Multiple implementations available.

github.com/jwagner Platform

Shadertoy

Write GLSL shaders in the browser. A playground for noise functions, ray marching, procedural textures, and GPU art.

shadertoy.com Library

Processing

The original creative coding environment. Java-based desktop IDE with a massive ecosystem of libraries for sound, video, and hardware.

processing.org

📖 Learning & Tutorials

Book

The Book of Shaders

Patricio Gonzalez Vivo's gentle, step-by-step guide to GLSL fragment shaders. Covers everything from gradients to fractals to noise.

thebookofshaders.com Book

On Generative Algorithms

Inconvergent's deep dive into generative algorithms — flow fields, spatial subdivision, organic structures, and more.

inconvergent.net/generative Book

The Nature of Code

Daniel Shiffman's classic — simulate natural systems with code. Particles, forces, autonomous agents, fractals, and neural networks.

natureofcode.com Videos

The Coding Train

Daniel Shiffman's YouTube channel. Joyful, accessible tutorials on p5.js, generative art, machine learning, and creative coding challenges.

youtube.com/c/TheCodingTrain Article

Noise Functions (Inigo Quilez)

The definitive guide to gradient noise, simplex noise, and their variants. Mathematical rigor with beautiful visual explanations.

iquilezles.org Videos

Sebastian Lague

Beautifully produced videos on algorithms, procedural generation, terrain, and simulations. High production value meets deep content.

youtube.com/@SebastianLague
